Who We Are

Our Story & Mission

Discover the journey and vision of She Can Foundation, an organization dedicated to empowering women and creating social transformation.

The Origin

How It Started

It began as a grassroots effort led by Reeta Mishra, after witnessing the immense challenges underprivileged girls faced in accessing basic menstrual hygiene resources. Seeing girls drop out of school due to lack of facilities and awareness, she brought together a passionate team to provide Dignity Kits and run community workshops, laying the foundation for what is now a registered Indian Society.

Our Mission

What is She Can?

Registered under the Indian Society Act of 1860, She Can Foundation is a dedicated force for women’s upliftment. Through menstrual dignity campaigns, vocational skill-building, and leadership development, we target root-level systemic barriers. Our work ensures that every woman and girl we reach gains the agency, education, and resources to build an independent life.

Transparency & Legitimacy

Indian Society Registration Certificate

We are proud to be registered under the Indian Society Act, 1860, and we are committed to operating with complete transparency and accountability.

Our registration is a testament to our dedication to creating positive change in the world and to our commitment to empowering women. As a registered non-profit organization, we are held to a high standard of integrity and professionalism, and we take that responsibility very seriously.
