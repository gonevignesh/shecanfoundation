MENSTRUAL HYGIENE INITIATIVE

"Her Period Shouldn't End Her Education!"

Dear Donor,

Every month, a 13-year-old girl in a small village is forced to miss five days of school. Not because she doesn’t want to learn, but because she cannot afford something as basic as a sanitary pad.

She hides at home in shame. She uses rags, newspapers, even sand — risking her health and her dignity. Slowly, her dreams of education, freedom, and self-respect begin to fade.

1 in every 5 girls in India drops out of school because of periods. Over 12 crore women and girls still lack access to proper menstrual hygiene. The result is shame, health risks, and lost opportunities. But you hold the power to change this.

At She Can Foundation, we have already helped 1,20,000+ girls across India with free sanitary pads, workshops, and dignity kits. For every 1 girl we reach, 5 more are still waiting. Be the reason she dreams again.
